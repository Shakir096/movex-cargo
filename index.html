About MoveX Cargo

Built by people who move parcels like their own.

MoveX Cargo started in 2011 with two vans in Rawalpindi and a simple promise: treat every parcel like it's our own. Today we deliver door-to-door from Rawalpindi and Islamabad to the US, UK, Canada, Europe, and the Gulf — but that same promise drives every parcel we handle.

Our Mission

To make sending a parcel abroad as easy as sending one across town — pickup from your door, delivered to theirs, no paperwork headaches.

Our Vision

To be Pakistan's most trusted door-to-door international parcel service — known for on-time delivery and clear pricing from Rawalpindi and Islamabad to the world.
